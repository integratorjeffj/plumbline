\#\# Company Overview

Crestmark Construction Partners is a fictional regional commercial general contractor and construction-management firm headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, with satellite operations in Charlotte, North Carolina and Nashville, Tennessee.

The company specializes in healthcare, higher education, municipal, and complex occupied-facility construction across the Southeast. Its positioning is intentionally believable rather than flashy: mature enough to have formal preconstruction and procurement processes, but not so large that every workflow is already solved by custom enterprise software.

\#\#\# Working profile

\- Founded: 2004  
\- Headquarters: Atlanta, Georgia  
\- Regional offices: Charlotte, NC and Nashville, TN  
\- Employees: approximately 240  
\- Annual construction volume: approximately $425M  
\- Typical project size: $5M-$80M  
\- Delivery methods: CM at Risk, Design-Build, General Contracting  
\- Core markets: Healthcare, Higher Education, Municipal, Commercial Interiors

\#\# Procurement Workflow

The fictional current-state process should be believable and somewhat manual:

1\. Crestmark issues bid invitations to prequalified subcontractors.  
2\. Vendors submit proposals by email in inconsistent formats.  
3\. The Preconstruction Coordinator saves files to the project record.  
4\. Estimators manually compare totals, exclusions, allowances, alternates, and scope.  
5\. Clarification questions are sent by email.  
6\. Revisions are manually tracked.  
7\. The estimator prepares a bid-leveling worksheet and recommendation.  
8\. Final selection remains a human decision.

The Bid Intelligence & Procurement Copilot is intended to compress and improve steps 2-7 without removing human accountability.

\#\# Product Narrative

Crestmark is not portrayed as dysfunctional. The company has a normal, mature process that still requires significant manual review because subcontractor proposals are inherently inconsistent.

The product should therefore be positioned as:

\*\*A procurement intelligence layer that helps experienced construction professionals review more accurately and efficiently, while preserving source evidence and human judgment.\*\*

\#\# Phase 1 Constraint

\- enough branding to make generated documents credible
