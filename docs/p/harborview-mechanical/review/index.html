SCOPE OF WORK
The following scope is included in our Base Bid of $398,500.00:
1 Mechanical mobilization and on-site supervision for the duration of our work.

4 Hydronic piping and specialties serving the mechanical rooms and air-handling equipment.
5 Integration of our equipment with the building automation system (BAS) specified in Section 23 09 00.
